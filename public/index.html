EASY EARN — Privacy Policy

EASY EARN collects only what it needs to run your account: your name, email, password (stored as a hash, never in plain text), referral information, device/IP fraud signals, and your earning and withdrawal ledger. Your wallet balance is derived from that ledger, never stored as a single editable number.

We do not sell your data. Withdrawal account details you enter are used only to process your own withdrawal request and for admin verification of that request. Admin payout-source details, where configured, are never shown to regular users and never shown in full to anyone.

There is no joining fee and no guaranteed income. Support contact details must be replaced with real operator details before a public launch — the placeholder contact in the full policy document is not a real support address.

Full text: see docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md in the source package.

EASY EARN — Terms of Use

By using EASY EARN you agree that: (1) you will use one genuine account and will not create fake accounts, self-refer, or submit false scores; flagged accounts may be reviewed, flagged or banned by an admin; (2) earnings are variable, depend on real partner availability, and are never guaranteed; (3) a withdrawal request is not a payment — it is paid only after an admin confirms a real external payout with a payout reference, and rejected requests refund locked coins; (4) offerwall and ad rewards depend on partner verification (signed postbacks / AdMob SSV) and are not credited on unverified claims.
